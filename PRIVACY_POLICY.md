Privacy Policy for OL FreeCell

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Thank you for playing OL FreeCell. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains — in plain English — how the app handles your information. The short version: OL FreeCell does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you.

Information We Collect

We collect nothing. OL FreeCell does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal detail. There are no accounts to create and no sign-in. The app has no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Game Data on Your Device

OL FreeCell saves ordinary game information so your experience stays smooth — things like your current game in progress, your daily challenge completion records, your winning streak, and basic statistics. This data is stored on your own device and, if you use iCloud, may be synced privately across your Apple devices through your personal iCloud account. We never see this information, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else. It stays under your control within Apple's ecosystem.

Information We Share

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any information, because we do not collect any in the first place. There is no data to hand over to advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Children's Privacy

OL FreeCell is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects nothing from children either.

Network Use

OL FreeCell is designed to be played offline. It does not require an internet connection to deal a game, and it does not transmit your activity to any server operated by us.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Since the app's approach is simply to collect nothing, we do not expect meaningful changes.
